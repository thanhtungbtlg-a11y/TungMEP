TUNG MEP FOR REVIT 2024
=======================

CAI DAT
1. Dong Autodesk Revit 2024.
2. Chay TungMEP_Setup_1.8.2_Revit2024.exe.
3. Mo lai Revit 2024 va tim tab Tung MEP.
4. Neu may co pyRevit, bo cai tu dong tao tab rieng "Tung MEP QA" voi cac panel
   "Project QA" va "pyRevit Companion". Tab rieng nay khong anh huong cac tool C#
   tren tab "Tung MEP" khi dung pyRevit Reload.

DUONG DAN CAI DAT
- Chuong trinh: %LOCALAPPDATA%\TungMEP\Revit2024
- Revit manifest: %APPDATA%\Autodesk\Revit\Addins\2024\TungMEP.addin
- pyRevit Companion (tuy chon): %APPDATA%\pyRevit\Extensions\TungMEP.extension

GO CAI DAT
Mo Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps, tim "Tung MEP for Revit 2024" va chon Uninstall.

LUU Y
- Bo cai nay chi danh cho Autodesk Revit 2024 64-bit.
- File chua co chu ky so. Windows co the hien canh bao Unknown Publisher.
- Dong Revit truoc khi cai dat, nang cap hoac go cai dat.
- Smart Auto Tag MEP can project co san Pipe Tag Type. Noi dung System/DN/Slope/BOP
  duoc dieu khien boi Tag Family dang chon. Tham so BOP tuy chon mac dinh la
  TungMEP_BOP_FFL va co the doi trong hop thoai.
- Universal Auto Tag MEP can project co san Tag Family Type cho tung category da chon.
  Tool chi xu ly family instance trong model hien tai va khong xoa tag thu cong.
- Smart Annotation Arrange sap xep Independent Tag va Text Note theo cac che do
  Align, Distribute, Compact, Smart Rail va Reflow. Preview co the Apply hoac huy
  de khoi phuc toan bo vi tri ban dau.
  Co the tu chia tag thanh hai rail trai/phai theo cum nguon hoac giu nhom
  trai/phai hien tai de tao bo cuc chu thich hai ben thiet bi.
- Documentation Rail tim nhieu rail, vi tri va thu tu de giam leader giao nhau,
  tranh chu de len model. Co the tat tuy chon optimize de dung lai cach bo tri cu.
- Smart MEP Annotation Pack co tuy chon tao native dimension den grid gan nhat va
  dimension cao do den level trong section/elevation neu co Revit reference hop le.
- MEP SLIPT BY LEVEL cat Pipe, Duct, Conduit va Cable Tray tai cac Level duoc chon.
  Cac vi tri Level cham fitting/accessory se duoc bo qua theo khoang an toan da dat.
  Co the tao union fitting tai cac diem cat neu Routing Preferences ho tro.
- Panel TungMEP Control gom Repeat Last TungMEP va Reload TungMEP.
- MEP Align, MEP Copy va Quick Select da duoc go khoi ribbon va launcher.
- More Tools co Align Objects by BOP va Equal Spacing de can cao do day va gian deu
  Pipes/Ducts/Conduits/Cable Trays cung cac family MEP duoc ho tro.
- VG Filter from Selection tao Parameter Filter native tu category va gia tri parameter
  cua doi tuong dang chon; co the ap dung ngay vao active view.
- Schedule from Selection tao Schedule native cho mot category, them cac field huu ich
  va co the loc theo mot parameter co cung gia tri tren tat ca doi tuong da chon.
- Sheet Renumber tu dong danh dau cac Sheet da preselect, co cay thu muc theo prefix
  Sheet Number, tim kiem nhanh va van giu bang preview so moi.
- View & Sheet Automation hien live preview ten View, so/ten Sheet va nhom viewport
  truoc khi tao; ten trung duoc mo phong dung theo quy tac them hau to cua tool.
- Grid Bubble Arrange dan cac bong bong Grid dang chong lan bang 2D leader trong view.
  Duong Grid model va native Grid reference khong bi di chuyen.
- pyRevit Companion la tuy chon. TungMEP van hoat dong day du neu may khong cai pyRevit.
  Panel Project QA co MEP Health Dashboard de quet connector ho, System Type, size,
  offset bat thuong, tag con thieu trong view va Revit warning lien quan MEP.
  Dashboard chi doc model va co link Select/Zoom tung phan tu; khong tu sua hoac xoa.
  Companion cung cap launcher tim kiem nhanh, Repeat Last, Reload Payload va Diagnostics.
